# Lexora 5.1 product brief

## Outcome

Make vocabulary discovery feel continuous: learners can open known words wherever they appear, describe the collection they want in natural language, and confidently save personal notes.

## Scope

- Recognized dictionary words in rendered learning content become accessible inline links.
- Lists gains an AI-assisted collection composer with preview and explicit save.
- Word notes gain a draft state, Save button, keyboard shortcut, and visible status.
- Existing local progress, notes, lists, imports, backups, and storage keys remain compatible.

## Non-goals

- No production deployment in this change set.
- No arbitrary web-page annotation or automatic import of words absent from Lexora's dictionary.
- No silent saving of AI output or note drafts.

## Acceptance

- Mouse, keyboard, and touch users can open linked dictionary words without nested controls.
- A prompt can produce a validated 5–20 word preview; only known unique words are saved after confirmation.
- Notes report Unsaved changes, Saved, Note removed, or a storage error accurately.
- Offline behavior, existing data, and the current Chromium/WebKit E2E suite remain intact.
